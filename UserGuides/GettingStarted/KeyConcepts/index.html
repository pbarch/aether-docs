Key Concepts¶

This page is a glossary or lexicon of the key concepts and terms used throughout Aethers Documentation

Node Controllers:¶

Node Controllers are the devices that Aether interacts with, each node controller can has a variety of Actuators and Sensors that they use to engage with the world around them.

Influence Engines:¶

Influence Engines spatial algorithms that can influence the behavior of a Node Controller and its Actuators. You can think of Influence Engines like virtual weather patterns that shift and change through space and time, like wind, clouds, waves, or ripples.

Read More Detail about Influence Engines Here

Subscriptions:¶

You can modify how sensitive the Actuators of a Node Controller are to an Influence Engine by modifying their Subscription to that Influence Engine.
